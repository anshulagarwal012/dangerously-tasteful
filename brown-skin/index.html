find your undertone first

the vein test

Look at the veins on your wrist in daylight. Green is warm. Blue or purple is cool. Can't really tell? You're neutral.

the paper test

Veins hard to see? Stand near a window and hold a white paper next to your face. Yellow or golden is warm, pink or rosy is cool, neither is neutral.

warm: earthy tones

They match the warmth in your skin.

warm: go off-white

Cream or off-white. Bright white can look a little harsh on you.

cool: blue-based

They bring out the cool undertone. A crisp bright white looks really cool too.

neutral: rich colours

You're lucky. Warm and cool colours both suit you, and rich ones like these look amazing.
