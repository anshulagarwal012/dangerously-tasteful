your perfume, all day

moisturise first

Dry skin makes the scent fade faster. Use a lotion that smells close to your perfume, or an unscented one so nothing clashes.

pulse points

Wrists, neck, behind your ears. They stay warm and let the scent out slowly. And don't rub your wrists: rubbing heats the skin, and the first notes fade faster.

skin, not just clothes

Clothes hold the smell longer, but the warmth of your skin is what opens it up.

read the bottle

More oil usually means it lasts longer. The formula matters too, so test it on your skin.

one extra: hair perfume

Hair holds scent really well. Use one made for hair, because the alcohol in regular perfume can dry it out.

save this

The idea isn't to smell strong the moment you walk in. It's to leave a scent people still notice after you've left.
