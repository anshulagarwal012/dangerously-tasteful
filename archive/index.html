Fashion Archive Edition

The four fashion archives from the reel, in the same order. Tap one to open it.

  1. 01 ARCHIVE.pdfarchivepdf.net ↗

    Free scans of rare old fashion magazines, lookbooks and catalogs, page by page. Plus interviews with designers.

    Start with the Raf Simons A/W 2002 "Virginia Creeper" catalog under Scans. It takes about 20 seconds to load.

    magazines · lookbooks · catalogs
  2. 02 firstVIEWfirstview.com ↗

    Runway shows look by look, from way before Instagram. Started by two runway photographers. Free to browse.

    Try Photographs → Search designers by name → Helmut Lang → Spring/Summer 1998, Men

    shooting shows since 1995
  3. 03 Kyoto Costume Institutekci.or.jp ↗

    A fashion institute in Japan that collects over 300 years of Western clothes. Every piece comes with a write-up.

    Try Search by Date → 2000s → the 2006 Dior Homme suit by Hedi Slimane

    online archive: 1740s → 2020s
  4. 04 We Wear Culturegoogle arts & culture ↗

    The fashion section of Google Arts & Culture, with clothes from over 180 museums and archives, like the Met and the V&A.

    Try searching shirt, or zoom into the V&A's embroidered dragon robe until you see the stitches

    180+ museums & archives
