Which
shirt fit

made for your body

the four fits

Slim follows your shape. Regular is cut straight. Relaxed is a little roomy. Oversized is boxy, with a dropped shoulder.

lean or slim body

Go for a slim fit in your actual size. It follows your shape and gives you a really sharp look.

broad shoulders, slim waist

A slim fit can work on you too.

buttons pulling?

If the buttons start pulling across your chest, the slim fit isn't for you.

then go regular

A regular fit gives you more room up top.

stomach wider than chest

Go for a regular fit. It's cut straight from the chest down, so it doesn't stick to your stomach.

don't size up to hide it

Buying a size bigger only makes it look bigger. Get the right fit in your size.

relaxed & oversized

These are more of a style choice, for when you want that casual, laid-back look.

check the fit up top

Shoulders

The seam sits on the edge of your shoulder bone. Up on the shoulder is too small, down your arm is too big. Oversized drops it on purpose.

Buttons

They lie flat. No pulling, no X-shaped creases across the chest.

Collar

Buttoned up, one or two fingers slide in.

check the length

Sleeves

The cuff ends at your wrist bone.

Length

Untucked, the hem lands around the middle of your zip.

Size chart

Sizes change from brand to brand. Check the chest and length numbers, not just S / M / L.

pick the fabric

Slim fit

A little stretch, cotton with some elastane, lets you move without the buttons pulling.

Indian heat

Linen breathes and dries fast, but it wrinkles. A cotton-linen blend wrinkles less.

Everyday

Cotton poplin is smooth and light, good for work. Oxford is thicker and more casual.
