Shirt
check 01

stitches don't lie

01 look

Along the collar and down the sides.

Well made

Tiny stitches, close together, perfectly straight.

Made cheaply

Big and wobbly, or loose threads hanging out.

On the sides

One neat line of stitching is the better way. With two lines, the fabric between them can wrinkle after a few washes.

02 pull

Hold the side, where the front and back are stitched together, and pull gently.

Well made

No gaps.

Made cheaply

Gaps open up between the stitches, because cheaper clothes use longer ones.

03 turn it inside out

Look at the edges, where the pieces are joined.

Overlock

Wrapped in loopy thread. The most common finish, and it does the job.

Flat-felled

Folded in and stitched down flat. One of the strongest, which is why jeans use it too.

French seam

Tucked inside a thin, neat fold. You'll find it on finer, lighter shirts.
