Wedding
colours

brown skin, haldi to reception

day

Haldi, mehendi, day wedding: soft, light colours. Pastels look really good on brown skin.

night

Sangeet, reception: darker and richer, in a fabric with a little shine like silk or velvet, because it catches the light.

any function

Anything close to your skin colour, and you and the outfit blend into one. Still want it? Throw a darker jacket or a dark stole on top.
